NEMO IMS

NEgentropic MOney International Monetary System

A monetary system in service of planetary regeneration

1

Regenerative money created without debt counterpart

The current system rests on a "curse": money is created by commercial banks against debt. This mechanism imposes a short-term profitability constraint that inexorably pushes toward infinite growth of extractive and degenerative activities.

Learn more →
2

A dynamic quantitative theory of money (Flow vs Stock)

NEMO IMS breaks with the vision of money as a static stock or a scarce commodity like gold or Bitcoin. It is defined as a permanent dynamic flow with a lifecycle comparable to natural cycles.

Learn more →
3

A new monetary hegemon for international cooperation

The architecture of world trade forces nations into extractive competition to stabilise their trade balance and accumulate key currencies like the dollar. NEMO IMS proposes replacing this destructive economic war with cooperation for planetary robustness.

Learn more →
