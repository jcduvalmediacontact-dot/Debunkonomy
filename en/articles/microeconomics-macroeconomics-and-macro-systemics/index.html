The chessboard metaphor

This article introduces the distinction between microeconomics, macroeconomics and macro-systemics through the metaphor of a chessboard resting on a table.

The core idea is simple: modern economies optimize the movements of the pieces while progressively destroying the ecological and civilizational foundations that make the game itself possible.

NEMO IMS proposes a monetary architecture aligned with systemic robustness and the regeneration of living systems.
